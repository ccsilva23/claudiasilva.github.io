Project Full Title: Immersive Nightmare Experience 
Short Name: Nexp and NexpAu
Co-authors: Melany Veliz, Aliyah Miller, Roberto Ortiz, Christopher Mai
Completed Date: 07/31/2026
Medium: Sculpture and audio 
Course Info: MAT 111 FS, Nefeli Manodaki
Abstract: The installation is an immersive, tent-like enclosure that the viewer physically enters and sits inside. It stages a descent from a dream state into an anxiety-nightmare state. The calm, organic contour projections shift both visually and sonically, into something more agitated and unstable. Moreover, a VR layer feeds in a live, distorted view of the outside world. a custom audio track I composed was fed into TouchDesigner, which analyzed in real time to drive the reactive projected visuals and the subpack’s haptic output.
